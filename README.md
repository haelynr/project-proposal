Problem: Many people have email inboxes with years of build up and clutter making it difficult for proper communication through email. 


What and Why: 
An app that goes through an email inbox and allows them to sort through which emails take priority to read and respond to as well as has tools to organize and clean an inbox from any subscriptions that are unused or creating clutter. This app will allow people to take the time to clean up their inbox for easier communication and organization. If they don't have the time to clean it, it will at least bring forward the most pressing emails to read and respond to. 

Ultimately this app should provide a way for users to filter their inboxes for a list of specific types of emails. 

How:
The project can utilize Google Cloud as a tool to be able to access emails. The user will first log into their emails using Google's OAuth 2.0 system. 

    Clutter / cleaning function:
        The app will identify the emails that have been send repeatedly or on a scheduled basis by certain companies. Users will get a list of the companies that are sending these types of emails and can habitually choose to archive or unsubscribe from these emails.
        The app should also be able to provide the user with a seamless way to unsubscribe from emails.

        The app should also be able to identify, based on the date an email was sent, which emails can be marked for decluttering or cleaning. If an email in the inbox has meet a certain threshold of time, then the user can choose to look through old emails and specify which ones should be marked as read or archived

    Prioritization function:
        The add should also be able to identify emails that might require a response. Based on the date it was send, and whether the email is asking for input or a reply, the app will show the user which ones they should look at or respond to. 

Scope:
    While the initial idea might sound like a simple scraping task of an email inbox, this project has a lot of capabilities to be expanded. One idea is incorporating an LLM to make the prioritization of emails more accurate. A function can also be added for the user to be able to prompt the app on what kind of emails they want to see a list of, whether that be emails from subscribed companies or emails related to work, school, or family. 

For Whom:
    This software will be used for the wide audience of people who use their email on a regular basis. Students who need to check their email for messages from a professor, impending due dates, or job application results would benefit from this.

    Professors who get emails from their students, bosses would benefit from this.

    Any business owner who gets emails from investors, employees, and more, would benefit from this.

    The end-user audience is wide ranging since many people have the same problem of a cluttered email.
